Could the Universe Exist Without Dark Matter?

In his GDS research, Martin Štěpánů reproduced the effects under study that are attributed to dark matter and dark energy using the dynamics of a single gravitational field, without introducing either component into the model. In galaxy rotation-curve tests, the field’s nonlinear response to ordinary matter generates an effective halo with the characteristic behavior of NFW dark matter halos.
