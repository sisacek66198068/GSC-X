Spectral Observatory — Strategic Technology Opportunity

A standalone scientific technology branch available for licensing, strategic partnership or technology transfer.

Spectral Observatory is an independently developed scientific and technological branch of the GSC-X ecosystem focused on autonomous astronomical spectral analysis, systematic processing of large observational datasets and identification of scientifically relevant candidates.

Following successful completion of large-scale production validation, the technology is offered for further development through technology licensing, strategic partnership or a complete transfer of the technology and associated know-how.

We welcome discussions with organizations active in astronomy, space research, scientific data platforms and advanced analytics.
